Korean indie electronic pop with percussive keys, 180 BPM in E Minor, an even straight 4/4 quarter-note kick and crisp snare maintaining a running cadence. Staccato electric piano and a restrained soft 808 sub-bass make a springy verse; short analog synth notes answer the lead. In the chorus open the chords and bass movement without a sudden drop, and keep a sixteen-bar instrumental space after each chorus. One male chest-led lead, clear Korean consonants and conversational timing, close vocal over a spacious but defined stereo mix. Sparse vocal phrases leave room for extended drum-and-synth instrumental passages.
