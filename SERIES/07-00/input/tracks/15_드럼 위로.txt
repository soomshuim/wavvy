=== STYLE ===
Confident Korean indie electro-rock, 180 BPM in F Major, straight 4/4 step-aligned kick and live snare with a solid rounded bass. Bright clean electric guitar and compact synth arpeggios share a rhythmic verse; choruses spread into wide guitar chords and a strong upper synth hook without a club drop. Strip the bridge to kick, bass and voice, then give the last chorus its fullest arrangement. One female chest-led lead, direct natural Korean diction and a clear strong midrange, no group vocals, polished organic mix. Sparse vocal phrases leave room for extended drum-and-synth instrumental passages.
