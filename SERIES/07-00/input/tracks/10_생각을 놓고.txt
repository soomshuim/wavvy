=== STYLE ===
Korean indie dance-pop, 180 BPM in D Major, consistent straight 4/4 quarter-note kick and snare with clipped synth chords and light hi-hat motion. A melodic synth bass adds playful anticipation without masking the beat; clean guitar notes answer the ends of phrases. Keep the verses direct and narrow, enlarge the chorus with brighter open chords, then strip back to the beat and bass in the bridge. One clear male chest-voice lead with natural Korean diction, dry and upfront against a polished room sound. Sparse vocal phrases leave room for extended drum-and-synth instrumental passages.

=== EXCLUDE ===
choir, backing vocals, falsetto, heavy 808, half-time drums, oversized EDM drop
