=== STYLE ===
Airy Korean indie synth pop, 180 BPM in D Major, an even straight 4/4 quarter-note kick and crisp snare that keep the running cadence despite a gentle surface. A soft plucked synth motif, warm bass guitar and sparse clean electric guitar make a clear melodic pocket. Choruses gain an extra bright keyboard layer and moving bass rather than a drum drop; hold one open instrumental break and return to the small opening motif in the outro. One male chest-voice lead, conversational Korean articulation, intimate but energetic mix. Sparse vocal phrases leave room for extended drum-and-synth instrumental passages.

=== EXCLUDE ===
choir, backing vocals, falsetto, heavy 808, half-time feel, ambient beatless section
