Type: B
BPM: 180
Key: B Minor
Vocal: Female
Length: 3min+

=== STYLE ===
Korean indie electronic rock, 180 BPM in B Minor, straight 4/4 running beat with crisp kick and snare on a steady forward pulse. Muted clean guitar chops interlock with a pulsing analog synth bass; a bright arpeggiator keeps the minor key curious rather than dark. Open the chorus with fuller guitar chords and a simple synth counterline, then leave two sixteen-bar instrumental spaces for the drums to carry the pace. One female chest-led lead with clear Korean syllables and a dry intimate tone. Sparse vocal phrases leave room for extended drum-and-synth instrumental passages.

=== EXCLUDE ===
choir, backing vocals, falsetto, heavy 808, half-time drums, metal guitar
