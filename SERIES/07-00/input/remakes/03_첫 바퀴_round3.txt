=== STYLE ===
Instrumental driving electroclash and motorik indie rock, full-speed 180 BPM in A Major. Relentless quarter-note kick, dry cracking snare and nonstop fast sixteenth-note closed hats establish an unmistakable running step from the first bar and continue through the whole song. Angular muted guitar and clipped synth chords lock to a tight octave bass, while a glassy high arpeggiator repeats the bright hook of First Lap. The refrain hits with wider guitar chords, sharp synth stabs and harder low end without reducing the drum rate. Strip back only the harmony in the bridge; the full-speed drums never stop before the final band lift. Punchy, urgent and sunlit, around three minutes.

=== EXCLUDE ===
vocals, spoken words, half-time drums, slow intro, beat-free breakdown, trap groove, oversized EDM drop
