## Lyric Strategy

- narrator/listener: one woman in bed at the end of the day, thinking of a former love and thanking the memory itself rather than addressing him.
- connection: the blanket pulled to her chin and the cold sheet taking her warmth (Verse 1) lead to a remembered sofa nap and the blanket put over her (Verse 2), her drowsiness (Pre-Chorus), and the Chorus line `그날도 이랬어`, which says today's warmth is the same as that day's; Verse 3 says the thanks she never said, and the Bridge hears that day say today was enough.
- emotional_movement: the body loosens, the old warmth arrives, the thanks are finally said (`고마웠어 이제야`), and the Final Chorus turns it into gratitude for the day itself (`그날이 고마워`). No sadness, regret, or plea.
- short_line_policy: 젠 asked for short sentences (「전체적으호 문장이 길지 않아」). Every sung row is 4-7 Hangul syllables (see the Self-Gate for the measured average and longest row), close to the 숨 original's 5.8 average.
- hook_or_repetition_role: the hook is the title word sung twice, `포근히 포근히` (젠 「후렴이 중독성이 약하네」). Features used: (1) a same-grammar pair, `베개는 폭신해` / `이불은 포근해`; (2) matching endings, `-신해 / -근해`, and the near-rhyme of `폭신` and `포근`; (3) a repeated word, `포근히 포근히`; (4) the title word is the hook, opens the Chorus and returns as its last row; (5) 3+3 syllable beats in every row. The Chorus is five rows (hook, pair, memory row, hook) and returns word for word in the second Chorus; the Final Chorus adds one thanks row before the last hook. It shares no hook word or shape with 10 귤 (that Chorus is six long sentences about tangerine scent, a stove, and a halved fruit).
- structure: the 숨 original runs Verse -> Verse -> Pre-Chorus -> Chorus -> Interlude -> Verse -> Verse -> Pre-Chorus -> Chorus -> Bridge -> Instrumental -> Breakdown -> Final Chorus -> Outro. This song follows that order with Verses 1-2 before the Pre-Chorus, Verse 3 after the first Chorus, a Rhodes-and-voice Bridge, and an Instrumental stop before the Final Chorus. It keeps three Verses, so no structure exception is needed; the Pre-Chorus appears once, to keep the plan near the target length.
- density_and_breath: four-row Verses sit in six bars (one row per 1.5 bars at 66 BPM), the two-row Pre-Chorus in four bars, the five-row Chorus in eight bars, the four-row Bridge in four bars with Rhodes under the voice, then a two-bar instrumental stop. The four-bar Intro and Outro and the stop are vocal rests; a line break alone is not counted as breathing room.
- duration_plan: 60 bars in 4/4 at 66 BPM estimate 218.2 seconds (about 3:38) toward a 200 second target, close to the 숨 original (3:35). The first generation of this track ran 2:53 and 2:39 as audio; this plan does not measure or guarantee generated audio.
