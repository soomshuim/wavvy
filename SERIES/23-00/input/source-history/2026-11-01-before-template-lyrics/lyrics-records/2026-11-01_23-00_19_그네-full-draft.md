## Lyric Strategy

- narrator/listener: one woman remembering a quiet night on adjacent swings with a former love; past tense in the Verses and Bridge, one present-tense line at the end of the Final Chorus.
- connection: the chain squeak answered from the other swing (Verse 1), the sand and the first push (Verse 2), the forward-and-back count of the Chorus, the senses that arrive on the swing (Bridge: night air on the forehead, a laugh at the ear, the squeak as the only talk), and the memory that now rocks her the way the swing did.
- emotional_movement: a still, private night becomes a shared rhythm and then a comfort that is still present (`그 밤이 아직도 / 나를 흔들어 줘`). No sorrow or plea; the minor key is carried by the arrangement, not by the words.
- short_line_policy: 젠 asked for short sentences (「전체적으호 문장이 길지 않아」). Every sung row is 4-7 Hangul syllables (see the Self-Gate for the measured average and longest row).
- hook_or_repetition_role: the hook is the three-row block `한 번 앞으로 / 한 번 뒤로 / 삐걱 삐걱 두 그네`, sung twice in every Chorus (젠 「후렴이 중독성이 약하네」). Features used: (1) a same-grammar pair, `한 번 앞으로` / `한 번 뒤로`; (2) matching endings, `-로 / -로` and `삐걱 / 삐걱`; (3) repeated words, `한 번` and `삐걱`; (4) the title word `그네` ends the hook and comes back at the Chorus end; (5) short even beats (2+3, 2+2, 2+2+3 syllables) that count the swing's rhythm. It does not copy the sound of 18: 18 is a petal-and-wind chant, this is a forward-and-back count with the chain squeak.
- structure: the 이름 original runs Verse 1 -> Chorus -> Verse 2 -> Bridge -> Chorus -> Outro. This song keeps two Verses and puts the Bridge before the Final Chorus, and it repeats the Chorus once after Verse 2 so the hook returns three times, matching the STYLE order. In the Final Chorus two new rows sit between the two hook blocks.
- density_and_breath: four-row Verses and the four-row Bridge sit in eight bars (one row per two bars at 70 BPM); the six-row Chorus sits in eight bars; the eight-row Final Chorus has twelve bars. The four-bar Intro and Outro are vocal rests; a line break alone is not counted as breathing room.
