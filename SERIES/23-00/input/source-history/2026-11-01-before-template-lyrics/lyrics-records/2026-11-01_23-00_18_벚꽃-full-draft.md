## Lyric Strategy

- narrator/listener: one woman on a bench under a cherry tree in a later spring, talking to herself; the former love appears only as `네` in the remembered touch.
- connection: a petal tapping her head now (`머리 위에 톡`) leads to the spot a hand once touched (`네 손이 닿던 자리`), Verse 2 says whose hand it was and how gently it lifted the petal, and the Bridge hears the yearly petals as a greeting she can answer.
- emotional_movement: light surprise becomes a remembered tingle and then settled comfort (`흩날려도 괜찮아 / 나도 잘 지내`). No grief, regret, or plea; the longing stays warm.
- short_line_policy: 젠 asked for short sentences (「전체적으호 문장이 길지 않아」). Every sung row is 5-8 Hangul syllables (see the Self-Gate for the measured average and longest row); each Verse row holds one sense and explains nothing.
- hook_or_repetition_role: the hook is the couplet `꽃잎이 가만가만 / 바람도 가만가만` (젠 「후렴이 중독성이 약하네」). Features used: (1) a same-grammar pair, `꽃잎이`/`바람도` + `가만가만`; (2) identical row endings (`-가만가만`) plus the closing pair `자리 / 간지러워`; (3) a repeated word, `가만가만`, four times per Chorus; (4) the key word `꽃잎` opens the hook and returns at the Chorus end, with the title word `벚꽃` opening Verse 1; (5) even 3+4 syllable beats in both hook rows. The Chorus is six rows (hook pair, two content rows, hook pair) and returns word for word; the Final Chorus is the same six rows plus the hook pair once more.
- structure: the 이름 original runs Verse 1 -> Chorus -> Verse 2 -> Bridge -> Chorus -> Outro. This song keeps two Verses and puts the Bridge before the Final Chorus, and it repeats the Chorus once after Verse 2 so the hook returns three times, matching the STYLE order (pulled-back bridge, then a gently fuller final chorus).
- density_and_breath: four-row Verses and the four-row Bridge sit in eight bars (one row per two bars at 78 BPM); the six-row Chorus sits in eight bars; the eight-row Final Chorus has sixteen bars. The four-bar Intro and the eight-bar Outro are vocal rests; a line break alone is not counted as breathing room.
- duration_plan: 68 bars in 4/4 at 78 BPM estimate 209.2 seconds (about 3:29) toward a 200 second target. The first generation of this track ran 3:05 and 3:00 as audio; this plan does not measure or guarantee generated audio.
- suno_handling: full Korean lyric source for Custom Mode Lyrics; whole-song sound, vocal, and instrument direction stay in STYLE, and only short section cues sit beside tags.
