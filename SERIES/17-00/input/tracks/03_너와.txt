=== STYLE ===
Korean acoustic neo-soul, 106 BPM, warm late-afternoon energy with a relaxed but forward-moving syncopated pocket. Fingerpicked and lightly strummed acoustic guitar sets the main rhythm; warm round bass answers the guitar, restrained live kick and snare sit behind a crisp shaker, and sparse Rhodes chords color the gaps. Keep the verses close and conversational, leaving space for natural Korean phrasing and the small back-and-forth of old friends. Single clear chest-dominant lead vocal, intimate and lightly playful, with steady pitch and natural diction. Let the pre-chorus add a little rhythmic lift, then open the chorus with wider guitar voicings, fuller bass movement, and a memorable melodic rise while the same solo lead stays upfront without backing harmonies. Briefly pull the instruments back in the bridge, then return to the groove for a warm final chorus. Organic, clean, vocal-forward mix; buoyant rather than sleepy, no melodrama.

=== EXCLUDE ===
choir, backing vocals, stacked harmonies, falsetto lead, airy whisper lead, glossy pop synths, trap hi-hat rolls, heavy 808, EDM drop, slow ballad pulse, cavernous reverb, distorted guitars
