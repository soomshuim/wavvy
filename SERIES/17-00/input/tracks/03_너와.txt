=== STYLE ===
Korean acoustic neo-soul, 106 BPM in G Major, with a warm, lightly syncopated late-afternoon sway. Begin with a distinct four-bar fingerpicked acoustic guitar intro before the vocal. Strummed guitar carries the groove, round bass answers its accents, soft live kick and snare keep it moving, and sparse Rhodes fills the pauses. One warm mid-low male lead sings in a clear, chest-led, conversational tone, close and natural. Verses leave room for the back-and-forth of old friends; the chorus opens into a memorable melodic lift with wider guitar chords and fuller bass, buoyant without a forced belt. Pull back to guitar and voice in the bridge, then return to a fuller final chorus. Aim for a complete song around 3:20, letting the intro and exchanges breathe. Intimate room ambience, clean separation, vocal-forward mix.

=== EXCLUDE ===
choir, stacked harmonies, falsetto lead, breathy whisper lead, heavy 808, EDM drop
